# Governed Scope Map

This map records project-owned scope meaning that current Vouch mk2 does not model itself. Each scope contract will be byte-bound into its Vouch unit acceptance pack. Inputs marked effectful or volatile prevent an unchanged implementation from implying a reusable real-world observation.

## Operator surfaces

- The server-rendered Portal invokes `manage-sender-installation`, `record-amendment`, `set-source-selection-policy`, `complete-reconciliation-snapshot` retry or Start Fresh Reconciliation, activation review and confirmed approve-and-activate or rejection, `produce-portable-export`, and `erase-retained-data` through their existing interfaces.
- The `portal` command invokes backup creation, restore, migration, reconciliation retry or Start Fresh Reconciliation, activation review and confirmed approve-and-activate or rejection, read-only Portable Export verification, and Portal Diagnostic Bundle production through those same governed interfaces.
- The Sender exposes local permission, sync, outbox, receipt, block, lifecycle, per-type Projection Refusal retry, Background Scheduling Status, and Diagnostic Bundle actions, but no health inspection or analysis. Expo BackgroundTask, foreground activation, the manual sync button, and the one-attempt retry control are adapters invoking the same durable synchronization interface. The Sender preserves an interrupted, collection-paused, or projection-refused type's exact saved position, attempts that position only when its governing condition permits, and starts a new Initial or Reconciliation Snapshot only if the source rejects it. The Portal may display `projection-refusal` and direct the Operator to the Sender but has no reverse command channel.
- No route or command owns a second implementation of governed behavior.

The Portal's stable primary destinations are Overview, Records, Summaries, Sources, and Operations. Overview leads with trust state scoped to the active Sender Installation: exact Last Delivery; separately labelled never-reported, current, delayed, or stale Polling Freshness from accepted Sync Checkpoint receipt; Sender backlog; processing and storage state; and visible conflict, quarantine, unresolved-policy, persistent reconciliation-failed, collection-disabled, or time-stamped last reported Background Scheduling Status. Never-reported is neutral setup state, gains first-poll-in-progress when an open run or accepted delivery exists, and becomes action-required only 24 hours after activation without a checkpoint. Enrollment time is separate and never stands in for a poll, and retired Sender state never makes a replacement appear healthy. Stale polling is prominent and directs the Operator to open the Sender and run a manual sync, but never invalidates retained health results. Overview follows with compact, provenance-aware cards for today's Steps, the latest completed Sleep session, the latest Weight observation, and the recent Heart Rate sample range; it does not add goals, scores, recommendations, or medical interpretation. Record detail owns provenance and Amendments, exposes Cutover Target, Candidate Build, and Abandoned Attempt Evidence with their identities and conditions, and never presents unpublished or abandoned evidence as current or contributing; Sources owns origin inspection and selection policy; Operations owns Sender state, the latest accepted Sync Checkpoint and paginated full checkpoint history, Reconciliation Failures and explicit retry, processing health, exports, and deletion. Summary views default to the most recent 30 local dates; offer inclusive 7-, 30-, and 90-date presets, a calendar-year preset, and inclusive custom date ranges; initially render daily points; preserve gaps and non-measured states; and never turn missing evidence into zero. A prior trusted value remains visible during ordinary refresh only with a newer-evidence-pending or refresh-failed condition, and during reconciliation with a reconciliation-pending or persistent reconciliation-failed condition. All browser reads and mutations rely on the same Tailscale-reachability trust assumption as ingress in v1, with CSRF protection for browser mutations but no account or login layer.
