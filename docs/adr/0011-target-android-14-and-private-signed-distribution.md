# Target Android 14 and private signed distribution

The first Sender will target Android 14 or newer and be delivered as a reproducibly built, privately distributed signed APK whose signing key is preserved; Google Play publication and older-Android compatibility are deferred until a concrete Device requires them. The Sender will include permanent enrollment, permission, connection, backlog, and manual-sync surfaces rather than behave as a headless transport. Its inventory distinguishes every catalogued type from types whose permission was explicitly granted and whose counts, origins, and ranges were actually read; the first governed delivery will exercise Steps, Heart Rate, Sleep Session, and Weight before Exercise Session. This narrows the platform, permission, and distribution burden for the single Operator while keeping the application honest and operable.
