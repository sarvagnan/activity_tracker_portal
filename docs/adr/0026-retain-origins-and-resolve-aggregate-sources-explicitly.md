# Retain origins and resolve aggregate sources explicitly

Records from different origins remain separate Canonical Revisions even when their values and times suggest the same real-world observation. Any duplicate grouping is reversible derived metadata with a stated rationale and confidence, never a destructive canonical merge. Every aggregate is bound to an append-only Source Selection Policy scoped by derived measure, applicable record type, stable origins, and an optional effective time interval; an all-time interval is the convenient default. One available origin may contribute automatically, but multiple overlap-capable origins or a newly encountered origin without an applicable rule leave the aggregate unresolved until the Operator selects one or explicitly permits a combination. Appending a requested policy and enqueuing every affected summary rebuild is atomic. The prior policy and results remain current while visibly marked as superseded or pending; one complete rebuilt result and its requested policy then activate atomically, or the prior state remains current on failure. All origins remain visible regardless of the choice. The Portal will not silently sum origins or label its results as Health Connect priority totals, because cross-origin identifiers and priority semantics are not generally reproducible from Raw Records alone.
