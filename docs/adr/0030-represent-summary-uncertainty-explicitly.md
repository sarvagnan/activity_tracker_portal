# Represent summary uncertainty explicitly

Derived summaries will distinguish a measured zero from missing coverage, missing required policy, processing failure, quarantined input, and unresolved overlapping sources, while carrying Source Conflict and similar evidence conditions separately from their primary state. A summary exposes its covered interval, Source Selection Policy, Fallback Timezone Policy when applicable, Processor Generation, Data Watermark, contributing evidence, and current, pending, failure, or uncertainty state. `Measured` means complete relative to retained processed evidence, not exhaustive real-world or future coverage; an open day is not structurally partial merely because more evidence may later exist. Compact views show the watermark's incorporated receipt time and pending or failed state, while details expose the exact ingest position, highest incorporated Sender sequence, evidence-set digest, and processing completion time; measurement time is never presented as the watermark. A view may keep a prior trusted result visible during conflict or rebuilding only when it is explicitly qualified as conflicted, superseded, newer-evidence-pending, reconciliation-pending, reconciliation-failed, or refresh-failed; an initial pending or failed result carries no number. Reconciliation-failed remains non-dismissible on every affected established result until a governed reconciliation succeeds or the affected retained data is explicitly deleted; disabling collection, retiring a Sender, or acknowledging the warning does not resolve lost continuity, while Operations retains the failed attempt. A reconciliation cutover may publish safe contributions with explicit partial, failed, quarantined, Policy-Pending, and unresolved conditions only after integrity and coverage are complete. It never substitutes zero or presents an obsolete result as clean current evidence merely to make a chart continuous. Polling Freshness and Last Delivery are separate operational states and never change summary validity. Views may render gaps differently for readability but cannot erase the underlying distinction. This complicates queries and presentation in exchange for preventing absence, disagreement, configuration gaps, or processing defects from being presented as health observations.
