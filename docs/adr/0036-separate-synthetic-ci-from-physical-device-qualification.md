# Separate synthetic CI from physical-device qualification

GitHub-hosted CI will use committed synthetic fixtures to cover every pinned Health Connect type and will never contain personal health payloads, device identifiers, credentials, or secrets in fixtures, evidence argv, or output. Native-adapter releases additionally require a local automated physical-device qualification covering representative instantaneous, interval, series, nested-session, nutrition, change, and retraction behavior that the available sources can exercise. It also exercises Expo BackgroundTask registration, callback delivery, system interruption and restart recovery, foreground and manual triggers, and durable resumption, while accepting that CI cannot prove real OS timing. Its harness emits only health-data-free types, counts, versions, and diagnostics. Vouch records only the spawned harness command and its exit-derived outcome; neither the Portal nor CI will claim that a later GitHub anchor reproduced the phone, authenticated the device, or bound a device report. A self-hosted device runner and broader physical coverage are deferred until measured release needs justify them. This preserves broad deterministic coverage and honest evidence boundaries without making a private physical device part of hosted CI.
