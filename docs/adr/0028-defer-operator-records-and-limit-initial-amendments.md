# Defer Operator Records and limit initial Amendments

Version 1 will not create manual health records. A future manually entered value will be an Operator Record with its own Raw Record, provenance, and canonical processing, not an Amendment and not a fabricated Health Connect record. Initial Amendments are append-only notes, labels, exclusions from default aggregates, and later re-inclusions. They target the stable Source Record identity rather than one Raw Record or Canonical Revision, so they survive source corrections and reprocessing; supersession explicitly references an earlier Amendment instead of overwriting it. Notes and labels change presentation and filtering only. Exclusion or re-inclusion atomically enqueues affected summary rebuilding without creating a Canonical Revision; the Source Record and full Amendment history remain inspectable and exportable. Deleting the Source Record also deletes its health-bearing Amendment history. Typed value and time overrides are deferred until their per-type semantics and interfaces are designed. This keeps the first mutation surface narrow while preserving an honest future distinction between original assertions and reinterpretation of source evidence.
