# Keep initial health summaries conservative and evidence-bound

Initial derived behavior will prefer visible uncertainty over opaque aggregation or invented continuity. Steps totals use one selected origin and become partial or unresolved for overlaps or unallocatable day-crossing intervals rather than reproducing Health Connect's private aggregation semantics; ranges sum only explicitly safe known-Steps contributions and expose coverage counts without averaging or projection. Heart Rate preserves every sample. After source selection, equal-time, equal-value samples across any eligible origins form a reversible Duplicate Group and contribute once, while equal-time different values are unresolved; exact aggregate sum, usable sample count, minimum, maximum, and sample-weighted mean use no interpolation, time weighting, or inferred resting rate. A Sleep Session remains indivisible and belongs to its end-offset or fallback-derived wake date. Same-class internal or cross-session stage overlaps union, uncovered session time remains unclassified, differently classified overlaps are unresolved, every session remains inspectable, and time never double-counts. Sleep ranges retain known asleep, awake or out-of-bed, and unclassified durations plus session and coverage counts without a sleep score. Weight preserves every selected-origin measurement. Equal-time, equal-value measurements across eligible origins form a reversible Duplicate Group; equal-time different values make the latest point unresolved rather than invoking an arbitrary tie-break. Any correction, retraction, exclusion, or deletion recomputes affected groups without Operator confirmation, while earlier summary revisions preserve their historical grouping while retained. Compact ranges plot the latest resolved measurement for each local day and expose the latest resolved point without averaging, interpolation, trend, BMI, or change judgment. A Source Conflict preserves the last trusted result only with an explicit conflict condition and neither unordered projection contributes. Range composition preserves usable measured contributions beside coverage and condition counts rather than converting gaps to zero, discarding valid days, or collapsing uncertainty into one arbitrary worst state. This produces fewer apparently complete metrics in exchange for results whose derivation the Operator can inspect from retained evidence.
