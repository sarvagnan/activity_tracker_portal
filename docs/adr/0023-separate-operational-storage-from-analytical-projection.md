# Separate operational storage from analytical projection

SQLite will remain the Portal's authoritative Operational Store for receipt idempotency, Raw Record durability, Canonical Revisions, Processing Job and Summary Refresh Job leases, Deletion Ledger enforcement, and point reads. It will also materialize rebuildable, versioned daily Health Summaries for Steps, Heart Rate, and Sleep plus the selected latest-local-day Weight point; each retains its policies, Processor Generation, Data Watermark, coverage, state, evidence conditions, and contributing evidence. The watermark binds the exact ingest position, highest incorporated Sender sequence, evidence-set digest, receipt time, and processing completion time; ordinary cards show the receipt time and status, while detail exposes the exact values. Published summary revisions, Cutover Targets, activated, failed, and superseded Candidate Builds, their terminal outcomes, and health-bearing job-attempt history remain inspectable only while their underlying evidence is retained, and privacy deletion erases every affected canonical, derived, build, and linked-attempt artifact in scope. Ordinary readers obtain one coherent current-state snapshot through a deep Operational Store interface backed by a single SQLite read transaction. Processor, policy, first Initial Snapshot publication, Replacement Baseline, and Reconciliation Snapshot activations atomically update their applicable source, canonical, summary, policy, or generation selectors, select one validated Candidate Build where applicable, and append a health-data-free Cutover Record rather than creating a global view generation for every received record; ordinary refreshes do not. Reconciliation activation may include qualified Reconciliation Absences and terminal non-success states but cannot override incomplete or uncertain coverage. Cutover Records remain indefinitely, survive health-data deletion, and participate in Operational Backups, Portable Export manifests, and allowlisted diagnostics without source identifiers or health values. Weekly, monthly, and custom views compose attributed local-date results at query time, retain usable measured contributions alongside counts for every non-measured state and evidence condition, and never claim an incomplete subtotal is complete. Weeks use ISO Monday-to-Sunday boundaries and months use calendar boundaries rather than server or browser timezone. No Health Summary becomes authoritative source evidence. Application code will reach SQLite through domain-oriented operations that preserve whole invariants, not through a generic CRUD repository, ORM portability layer, or least-common-denominator database interface; the first implementation may therefore be openly SQLite-specific. A future DuckLake integration will be a separate, derived Analytics Projection for scan-heavy aggregate and time-series queries. It must be rebuildable from retained Canonical Revisions, tolerate explicit staleness, and participate in privacy deletion before it can serve results. This keeps operational correctness and deletion authority in the relational store while preserving a genuine path to columnar analytics without pretending that SQLite and DuckLake provide interchangeable capabilities.
