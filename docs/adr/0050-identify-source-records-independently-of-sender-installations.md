# Identify Source Records independently of Sender Installations

A Source Record will be identified by source-system kind, origin, record type, and the source-assigned Source Identity, not by the Sender Installation that observed it. For Health Connect, `Metadata.id` is the Source Identity; optional `clientRecordId` and similarities in time or value are never sufficient identity. Every observing Sender Installation remains attached as provenance, so an identical Health Connect record reacquired after reinstall can join its existing history while a record from another store with a different identifier remains separate and may only enter the existing reversible duplicate-grouping process. Reconciliation Absence is never inferred across Sender Installations. This accepts the source identifier's uniqueness contract and possible cross-installation Source Conflicts in exchange for preserving Amendments and record history across a Sender reinstall without fuzzy or destructive merging.
