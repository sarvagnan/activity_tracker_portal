# Delay first summaries until the Initial Snapshot is complete

For the first-ever baseline, each permitted record type will archive and process Initial Snapshot pages incrementally, but ordinary summaries will expose no health value until the Portal durably accepts both its receipt-bound Snapshot Completion and the Change Completion that drains the per-type token captured before the first snapshot page. Every page in both traversals must reach a terminal processing outcome. When cutover work begins, its Cutover Target freezes the newest then-completed evidence boundary and an append-only Candidate Build holds its proposed outputs; later cycles wait for ordinary post-cutover publication rather than repeatedly restarting first publication. Delivery completion is then distinct from processing quality: safe contributions may publish with explicit quarantine, Policy-Pending, unresolved, or failed states and counts rather than leaving synchronization open forever. The first atomic publication creates a Cutover Record. Until then, Overview and Operations show health-data-free progress and an incomplete baseline; permission loss or missing completion evidence cannot be mistaken for an empty or complete history. Raw Records and completed Canonical Revisions remain immediately inspectable, and Portable Exports remain available only with explicit per-type completeness and pending-work metadata. A later Sender Installation's Initial Snapshot is instead a Replacement Baseline and cannot suppress the Portal's established view while it runs. This delays the first useful values and requires an end-to-end completion contract in exchange for preventing an arbitrary early page or snapshot race from being presented as the Subject's historical total or trend.
