# Keep raw records immutable only while retained

Raw Records remain unchanged while retained so processing can be audited and replayed, but an explicit deletion permanently removes matching raw, canonical, Amendment, Candidate Build, and derived health data. The Portal exposes deletion of one record, a source constrained by record type and time range, or the entire Portal. It first previews affected Raw Records, all canonical, candidate, and derived revisions, Amendment history, any whole-envelope collateral erasure, and server-retained Portable Exports that might contain the scope. The preview issues a short-lived, one-use token bound to that exact scope and current state; any intervening change invalidates it. Confirmation consumes the token, while whole-Portal erasure additionally requires re-entering a generated scope phrase. Destructive Sender retirement uses the same token-and-phrase discipline. Beginning deletion prevents new exports, cancels pending or running Export Jobs, and removes possibly affected completed server artifacts before a receipt; copies already downloaded outside the Portal are beyond its authority. It also atomically invalidates every active Cutover Target whose evidence intersects the deletion, cancels its outstanding leases, prevents all associated Candidate Builds from activating, and deletes their health-bearing outputs; only permitted health-data-free invalidation and Cutover Records may remain. Before erasing live data, the Portal durably appends a health-data-free instruction to a Deletion Ledger stored outside rotating health-data snapshots; restore uses an equivalent plan-then-confirm contract and refuses activation without the latest ledger. A deletion may otherwise leave only a health-data-free Deletion Receipt, Cutover Records, and non-reversible fingerprints of the exact erased Source Identities; those fingerprints suppress retries or historical rereads of the same identities without suppressing genuinely new records in the same time range, and affected summaries must be rebuilt. Queued work is re-evaluated against post-deletion state only after the receipt rather than resuming invalidated builds. A receipt is issued only after live operational and analytical data in scope is no longer available, and it states the backup-expiry deadline. Encrypted backups may retain deleted data for at most 30 days. Live deletion promises application-level removal and database scrubbing, not forensic erasure from SSD wear-leveling, deployment snapshots, or an unexpired encrypted backup. This chooses privacy deletion over an undeletable audit history while stating the storage boundary honestly and preventing erased data from being resurrected.
