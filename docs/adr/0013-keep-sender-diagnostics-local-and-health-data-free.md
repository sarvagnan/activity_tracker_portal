# Keep Sender diagnostics local and health-data-free

The Sender will have no third-party analytics, crash reporting, or automatic diagnostics upload. Its explicit Export Diagnostics action constructs an allowlisted, health-data-free Diagnostic Bundle locally and hands it to Android's save/share mechanism; Portal diagnostics are produced separately through the `portal` command. Both identify their origin and share a bundle contract, but neither scrapes arbitrary logs. The Sender issues local notifications only for Operator-action-required states such as permission loss, unusable key or screen lock, storage pressure, an oversized record, a Delivery Block, an incomplete lifecycle transition, restricted background scheduling, or unexpectedly missing task registration detected while the app runs; ordinary network retries and inexact scheduling delays do not notify. Foreground and manual synchronization remain available, and v1 does not request a battery-optimization exemption or introduce a foreground service. Each Sync Checkpoint reports the allowlisted Background Scheduling Status observed for that Polling Run; the Portal preserves its receipt time and never presents that historical observation as guaranteed current Device configuration. Real values remain locally inspectable by the Operator, while repository fixtures are synthetic. This trades effortless remote observability for a meaningful local-first privacy boundary without adding automatic upload or screen-capture controls.
