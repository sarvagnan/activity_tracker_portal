Fixed posture

Local authority without an account system.

Tailscale reachability establishes Authorized Ingress and browser trust in v1. That mechanism can later be augmented or replaced at the ingress seam without changing domain behavior.

One humanThe Subject and Operator are the same person; there are no profiles or accounts.
Reachability is trustAny device that can reach the Portal may read and mutate it in v1.
CSRF still appliesBrowser mutations resist hostile-site submission without claiming a login layer.
Deployment is separateServer hardening, availability, and tailnet configuration stay outside product scope.

Receipt-bound synchronization

Nothing leaves the phone on hope alone.

The Sender stores each encrypted Outbox Message until the Portal durably acknowledges its exact identity. Unknown outcomes retry identical bytes; an actionable mismatch blocks the head rather than skipping data.

Observable operation

One checkpoint per run

Every normally completed or recovered-interrupted Polling Run reports health-data-free per-type outcomes and scheduling state.

Honest recovery

No continuity fiction

An expired change token triggers a complete Reconciliation Snapshot; absence becomes evidence only under unchanged proven coverage.

Evidence and interpretation

Preserve first; interpret by version.

Raw Records remain byte-for-byte immutable while retained. Canonical Revisions are record-type-specific interpretations, and publication changes only through coherent governed cutovers.

Archive broadly

All stable SDK types

Every permitted Health Connect health-and-fitness type is projected and archived. The experimental Medical Records/PHR subsystem is outside scope.

Interpret carefully

Four initial summaries

Steps, Heart Rate, Sleep, and Weight receive conservative processors. Other understood types remain visibly Unmodeled.

Retain provenance

No destructive merge

Origins, observing Sender Installations, source changes, conflicts, retractions, Amendments, and prior interpretations remain inspectable.

Missing is not zero

Measured, partial, pending, failed, quarantined, policy-pending, and unresolved states remain distinct in every summary and range.

Policy is explicit

Overlapping origins need a Source Selection Policy; missing experienced offsets need a confirmed Fallback Timezone Policy.

Publication is coherent

Candidate Builds and immutable Cutover Targets prevent mixed processor versions or partially published synchronization cycles.

Product surface

Inspection before interpretation.

The first useful release archives every permitted type, exposes generic record evidence, derives four conservative views, and makes synchronization, export, and deletion operationally visible.

Server-rendered views

FastAPI remains a thin shell around governed Modules. Jinja views and focused interactions do not own health-state rules.

Durable local work

A separately supervised worker leases SQLite Processing, Summary Refresh, and Export Jobs. Celery waits for measured multi-machine need.

Operational trust state

Last Delivery, Polling Freshness, Sender backlog, scheduling status, conflicts, quarantine, storage, and reconciliation failures remain distinct.

Conservative ranges

Inclusive 7-, 30-, and 90-date windows and calendar-year/custom ranges preserve gaps and evidence conditions without medical advice.

Portability and recovery

Three artifacts, three contracts.

Synchronization, external portability, disaster recovery, and support evidence do not share one ambiguous “export” path.

Portable Export

A requested, database-independent ZIP/ZIP64 package with canonical JSON/NDJSON, schemas, manifest hashes, optional CSV, and a stable Package ID. It is not a v1 restore input.

Operational Backup

The encrypted, restorable SQLite state plus Deletion Ledger under a deployment-owned periodic retention workflow. This is the supported recovery path.

Diagnostic Bundle

A manually saved, health-data-free allowlist of versions, aggregate states, timings, aliases, and curated failure codes—never scraped logs.
